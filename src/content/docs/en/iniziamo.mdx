<AsideNote>
The screen is divided into **four panels**:
- <IconInline icon="up-arrow" />the **Top Panel**
- <IconInline icon="left-arrow" />the **Left Panel**
- <IconInline icon="document" />the **Center Panel**
- <IconInline icon="right-arrow" />the **Right Panel**
</AsideNote>

## <IconInline icon="up-arrow" />The Top Panel
The **top panel** contains:
- the logo
- the search box <IconInline icon="magnifier" />
- links to social media:
  - <IconInline icon="linkedin" />LinkedIn
  - <IconInline icon="youtube" /> YouTube
  - <IconInline icon="github" /> GitHub
- a button to **hide or show the right panel**
- a **theme** selector (Light/Dark) <IconInline icon="sun" />/<IconInline icon="moon" />
- a **language** selector (English/Italian) <IconInline icon="translate" />

## <IconInline icon="left-arrow" />The Left Panel
In this panel, you can **select the page** you are interested in:
- choose a section (Notes, Practical Labs, or Projects)
- choose a topic
- choose a page

## <IconInline icon="document" />The Center Panel
This is the **main panel**, which displays the **content of the selected page**.

## <IconInline icon="right-arrow" />The Right Panel
In this panel, you can see:
- your **quiz progress** (if the page contains a quiz)
- your **progress on the current page** and **select a section**.


### Expanding the Center Panel
If **diagrams in the center panel are difficult to read**, you can
**hide the right panel** to **expand the center panel**.
