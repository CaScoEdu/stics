Starvation vs. Deadlock: Key Difference

  • • Deadlock: Processes are mutually blocked waiting for each other's resources. No progress is made.
  • • Starvation: The CPU remains active and productive executing high-priority processes, while a low-priority process (P_low) is perpetually bypassed in the Ready Queue.

How Operating Systems Solve Starvation: Aging

To prevent indefinite blocking, the scheduler applies Aging: the longer a process waits in the Ready Queue without CPU time, the higher its priority becomes. Eventually, P_low reaches High Priority (1) and is guaranteed CPU execution.
